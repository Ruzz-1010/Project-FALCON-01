## Approved direction

Following DOST feedback and the latest visual reference, the active design direction is a smaller compact can-buoy with a round float body, tapered top housing, ESP32 local sampling, event-driven reporting, direct cloud connectivity, and a reduced battery.

## Architecture changes

- The ESP32 continuously samples locally but does not upload every raw reading.
- One-minute summaries are uploaded every 1–5 minutes during normal operation.
- Immediate event packets are reserved for significant pressure or movement changes, displacement, tamper, low battery, sensor faults, and connection recovery.
- Wi-Fi is the laboratory path. A 4G/LTE modem and SIM are the remote field path when no reliable shore Wi-Fi is available.
- Local flash or microSD buffering preserves data during Internet outages.
- No LoRa gateway, shore mini PC, or on-buoy computer is required for the reduced minimum build.
- Cloud hosting and a development computer are separate infrastructure costs.

## Sensor changes

- Pressure-derived wave sensing remains the core measurement.
- The preferred field interface is a low-range 4–20 mA submersible transmitter.
- 0–5 V/0–10 V, RS485/Modbus, marine digital, and protective-stilling-tube approaches are documented alternatives.
- Bar02 is bench-only unless its immersion and drying requirements are explicitly resolved.
- MPU6050 is the preferred low-cost movement/tilt sensor.
- GPS, wind, water temperature, tamper, and detailed power monitors are supporting or optional channels subject to adviser confirmation.
- BNO085, salinity/conductivity, and load-cell mooring tension remain excluded from the low-cost minimum build.

## Power changes

- Start with a 12.8 V 6–10 Ah LiFePO4 battery candidate.
- Start with a 10–20 W solar-panel candidate.
- Final sizing must use measured ESP32/modem duty cycle, LTE registration/transmit peaks, overnight deficit, and charging performance.
- No endurance or field-readiness claim is allowed before logged tests pass.
