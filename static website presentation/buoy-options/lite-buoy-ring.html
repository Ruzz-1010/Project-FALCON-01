Why a ring (vs can + catamaran)

  • Foam donut float unsinkable collar, no molding, widest waterplane of the three
  • Open moon pool waves pass through instead of lifting the hull — less heave
  • Pressure sensor hangs sheltered calm water inside the ring, easy to service
  • Trade-off: bulkiest to transport and the braced tower joint must be proven

Sensor placement

  • Wind head atop the pole slim pole straight into the hub — clean airflow
  • 3 open cups Ø110mm class compact head suits the small buoy
  • Wind vane 120mm below rotor speed + direction
  • Panel clamped on the pole compact mini solar panel set, final wattage after measured load
  • GPS puck + whip on deck kept clear of the panel

Buying note

  • Existing ESP32 kit, breadboard, jumper wires, and buck converters may be reused for bench validation, but replacement prices remain in the proposal budget.
  • Water pressure sensor must be confirmed first: low range, 4–20 mA output, IP68, cable type, and seawater/material compatibility.
  • Use Wi-Fi first for lab or near-shore tests; buy LTE only if the validation site has no reliable Wi-Fi.
  • Baseline power choice is 12.8V 10Ah LiFePO4 with BMS and 6× 3W panels; final runtime still depends on measured current draw.
