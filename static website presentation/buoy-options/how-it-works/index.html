What each beat shows

  1. 1 · Sea conditionWaves, wind and location are the conditions the buoy observes.
  2. 2 · Compact buoySmall body, mooring, pressure tube, wind head, GPS, solar and battery.
  3. 3 · Sensor samplingESP32 reads pressure, wind, GPS and power locally.
  4. 4 · Two-minute windowReadings are summarized as average, minimum, maximum, peak and status flags.
  5. 5 · Cloud packetOne compact packet goes through Wi-Fi/LTE instead of raw continuous uploads.
  6. 6 · DashboardThe cloud stores the packet and updates the panel-facing dashboard.
  7. 7 · Power validation12.8 V 10 Ah LiFePO4 battery and 18 W solar plan still require measured tests.

Daily arithmetic (design values)

Local sample interval≈ 5 s
Local samples per day≈ 17,280
Cloud packet interval2 min
Routine packets per day720
Packet contentavg · min · max · peak · flags
Network reduction≈ 24× vs raw uploads

If a high wave happens inside the two-minute window, it is kept as the window peak, not lost as a single last reading.

Still owed before approval
