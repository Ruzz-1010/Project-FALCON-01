Buying note

  • Existing ESP32 kit, breadboard, jumper wires, and buck converters may be reused for bench validation, but replacement prices remain in the proposal budget.
  • Water pressure sensor must be confirmed first: low range, 4–20 mA output, IP68, cable type, and seawater/material compatibility.
  • Use Wi‑Fi first for lab or near-shore tests; buy LTE only if the validation site has no reliable Wi‑Fi.
  • Baseline power choice is 12.8V 10Ah LiFePO4 with BMS and 6× 3W panels; final runtime still depends on measured current draw.

Validation checklist before fabrication

  • Mass, displacement, waterline, ballast, and righting check
  • Measured ESP32 + LTE current draw vs battery and solar target
  • Pressure stilling tube calibration and 2-minute summary test
  • Wind mast vibration check and GPS antenna placement check
  • Dry-fit, leak, flotation, heel, and recovery tests
