Why a catamaran (vs the can)

  • Two pontoon floats + deck off-the-shelf pipe/fittings, no molding, wide stable stance
  • Single 40W panel as cabinet roof tilted 10°, rails only — no heavy frame
  • Pressure sensor hangs between hulls protected from slap, easy to service
  • Trade-off: wider to transport, more fasteners best where stability matters most

Sensor placement

  • Wind mast at the deck rear slim Ø36mm pole, hub straight on top — clean airflow
  • 3 open cups Ø130mm, Ø600mm spacing was 200mm / Ø880mm
  • Wind vane 150mm below rotor speed + direction
  • Electronics inside the cabinet illustrative — clean deck, short wiring
  • Whip on the wind mast kept below the rotor plane
