Why a spar (vs Option A drum)

  • Cheapest hull off-the-shelf Ø250mm PVC pipe + fittings, no molding
  • Single 40W panel as cabinet roof tilted 12°, rails only — no heavy frame
  • Small collar float + bottom ballast self-righting spar behavior
  • Trade-off: taller profile, needs ballast + stability calc best for calm barangay waters

Sensor placement (same fixes as A)

  • Rotor on a slim Ø36mm mast hub straight on the pole — no fat tube in the rotor plane
  • 3 open cups Ø130mm, Ø600mm spacing was 200mm / Ø880mm
  • Wind vane 150mm below rotor speed + direction
  • GPS puck + LoRa + controller internal illustrative — clean topside per reference
  • Whip + LoRa SMA stub on rack kept clear of the rotor plane
