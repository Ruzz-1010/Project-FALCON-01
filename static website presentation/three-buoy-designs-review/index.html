Tamper/motion and temperature sensors are optional. Do not buy Raspberry Pi, LoRa/Bay Station, camera or water-quality sensors for the minimum build.

02 / DESIGN OPTIONS

Three body concepts

OPTION A · CURRENT PROPOSAL RECOMMENDATION

Compact can buoy

Size: Ø840 mm × 500 mm
Height to wind sensor: about 930 mm

Body: UV-stabilized HDPE.

Best for: smallest body, easiest transport, fewer parts.

Must test: ballast, freeboard and righting.

OPTION B · WIDE PLATFORM BACKUP

Catamaran buoy

Size: about 1,500 × 1,100 × 1,000 mm
Pontoons: Ø220 mm × 1,500 mm

Body: two rigid HDPE/PE pontoons or marine-FRP floats; 316/protected aluminum frame.

Best for: wide platform, easier access, drainage deck.

Included: open-bottom stilling tube between pontoons.

Must test: frame joints, torsion and mooring bridle.

OPTION C · RING BACKUP

Ring buoy

Outside diameter: 1,100 mm
Ring tube: 260 mm
Height to wind sensor: about 900 mm

Body: closed-cell marine foam inside HDPE or marine-FRP shell; structural center frame.

Best for: wide ring and centered mast/mooring.

Must test: center frame, foam protection and moon-pool response.

04 / BEFORE FABRICATION

Validate before fabrication

  1. Select one option. Do not fabricate all three or buy duplicate modules.
  2. Measure the real parts. Check battery, enclosure, sensors, panels, connectors and mounts.
  3. Float-test the complete body. Record mass, waterline, freeboard, center of gravity and righting behavior.
  4. Test pressure and power. Run 4/12/20 mA tests and a 24-hour power log; include LTE peaks if LTE is selected.
  5. Test water protection and mooring. Leak-test the enclosure, glands and stilling tube; check ballast, rated mooring and recovery plan.

05 / FINAL SLIDE — EQUIPMENT AND BUDGET

What we will use and the proposal budget

Electronics and sensors

  • ESP32 DevKit
  • Low-range 4–20 mA pressure transmitter
  • ADS1115 + 150 Ω shunt + protection
  • Wind speed and direction sensor
  • GPS / GNSS module
  • INA219/INA260 power monitor
  • microSD + card or tested flash buffer

Power and internet

  • 12.8 V 10 Ah LiFePO₄ battery with BMS
  • 6 × 3 W solar panels
  • LiFePO₄-compatible solar charger
  • Fuse, switch, regulators and protected wiring
  • A7670E/SIM7600 LTE module + LTE antenna
  • Active SIM card + data plan
  • Wi-Fi for laboratory / near-shore backup

Body and protection

  • One selected buoy body: A, B or C
  • Sealed electronics enclosure with gasket
  • Cable glands, connectors and tinned wire
  • Pressure stilling/protection tube
  • Ballast, brackets, 316 fasteners and reflective label

Mooring and safety

  • Anchor or deadweight
  • Chain, marine rope, shackles, swivel and thimble
  • Recovery line / marker
  • Flashing buoy beacon
  • Sealant, leak-test and calibration materials
