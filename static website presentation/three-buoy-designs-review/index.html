PROJECT FALCON-01 · DOST DESIGN REVIEW

Three Proposed
Buoy Designs

A short mobile-ready comparison of the compact can, catamaran and ring buoy concepts.

Decision needed: choose one design direction for detailed measurements, quotations and controlled prototype testing. None is fabrication-ready or field-deployed.

01 / SHARED SYSTEM

What stays the same in every option

Required sensing

  • 1× low-range 4–20 mA pressure transmitter
  • ADS1115 + 150 Ω shunt + protection
  • Wind speed and direction set
  • GNSS / GPS

Power and data

  • ESP32 controller
  • 12.8 V 10 Ah LiFePO₄ battery
  • 6× 3 W solar panels as starting assumption
  • Battery/solar monitoring and local buffer

Protection

  • Gasketed dry enclosure
  • Correct glands and tinned wiring
  • Stilling / protection tube
  • Ballast plus rated mooring hardware
Conditional only: LTE module + SIM when the trial site has no reliable Wi-Fi. Tamper/motion and temperature sensors are optional. Raspberry Pi, LoRa/Bay Station, camera and water-quality sensors are outside the minimum build.

02 / DESIGN OPTIONS

Three body concepts

OPTION A · CURRENT PROPOSAL RECOMMENDATION

Compact can buoy

Main body: Ø840 mm × 500 mm
Visible top equipment: about 930 mm above the model waterline

Candidate body: UV-stabilized HDPE. Use a gasketed enclosure, corrosion-resistant brackets and a side stilling tube.

Why consider it: fewest main parts, smallest transport footprint and lower fabrication complexity.

Validate: internal fit, ballast position, freeboard and righting behavior.

OPTION B · WIDE PLATFORM BACKUP

Catamaran buoy

Overall: about 1,500 × 1,100 × 1,000 mm
Two pontoons: Ø220 mm × 1,500 mm

Candidate body: two rigid HDPE/PE pontoons or engineered marine-FRP floats, with protected aluminum or 316 stainless crossmembers.

Current model: drainage deck, solid equipment mounts and an open-bottom pressure stilling tube between the pontoons.

Validate: pontoon/frame joints, torsion, uneven loading and bridle loads.

OPTION C · RING BACKUP

Ring buoy

Outside diameter: 1,100 mm
Ring tube: 260 mm
Mast/head: about 900 mm above the model waterline

Candidate body: closed-cell marine foam within a protective HDPE or marine-FRP shell, plus a structural center frame.

Why consider it: broad waterplane with centered mast and mooring concept.

Validate: center frame, braces, foam protection and moon-pool pressure response.

04 / BEFORE FABRICATION

Validation checklist

  1. Select one option. Do not fabricate all three or buy duplicate electronic modules.
  2. Freeze real purchased parts. Measure battery, enclosure, sensor, panel, connector and mounting sizes.
  3. Calculate and float-test. Record total mass, waterline, freeboard, center of gravity and heel/righting behavior.
  4. Verify sensing and power. Test 4/12/20 mA pressure inputs and log a full 24-hour power profile, including LTE peaks if LTE is selected.
  5. Test protection and mooring. Leak-test modified enclosures and glands; inspect stilling tube, ballast retention, rated mooring and recovery plan.
Meeting outcome to request: select Option A, B or C and approve detailed design planning. Field deployment requires the validation checks above.
