      Phase 1 — Undergraduate Prototype · Fullbright College

      Limited access to affordable coastal wave and wind data in barangay-level settings. Commercial buoys are costly, proprietary and maintenance heavy. Phase 1 answers that with a local-first, transparent and evaluable prototype. It senses underwater pressure and wind at sea; the shore turns those readings into estimated wave height and clear, usable information.

      01 / THE OCEAN

      01 / OBJECTIVES

      Six objectives.
      One Phase 1 prototype.

      Phase 1 is integration and evaluation, not sensor invention. These are the six objectives that frame the work.

      PHASE 1 OBJECTIVES
      1. Design a serviceable solar-powered buoy with passive mooring.
      2. Acquire pressure and wind measurements with supporting telemetry.
      3. Estimate wave height from underwater pressure with explicit calibration.
      4. Transmit via LoRa to a shore Bay Station for storage, processing and dashboard.
      5. Evaluate accuracy, reliability, latency, power use and usability.
      6. Develop and evaluate short-term AI wave prediction against a baseline.

      IN PLAIN WORDS These are the promises we are holding ourselves to — including the last one, which says we will check the numbers before we believe them.

      02 / HARDWARE BASELINE

      Built to listen.

      This is FALCON-01.
      One sensing buoy, linked to a shore station.

      On the buoy: ESP32 controller, underwater pressure sensor, wind speed and direction, GPS for position, time and geofence, battery with solar charging, and a LoRa transceiver. On shore: a mini-PC Bay Station with LoRa receiver, local storage and processing. The heavy work stays on land — the buoy only senses and sends.

      IN PLAIN WORDS A buoy is a floating device that sits on the water and collects information. It is not a full computer on the sea — the heavy processing happens on shore.

      08 / AI PREDICTION REQUIREMENT

      Observe the past.
      Estimate what comes next.

      Target: short-term wave-height prediction 5, 10 and 15 minutes ahead. Currently a transparent trend baseline. Evaluation requires calibrated data, a chronological train / validation / test split, and MAE, RMSE and bias reporting. This is not a trained model result yet.

      IN PLAIN WORDS An AI model is a program trained on past data to estimate one future value. Ours is a target, not a finished result.

      01 · WHERE DATA COMES FROMBay Station historyThe shore computer stores every reading. The predictor looks at the last 120 wave-height records and waits until there are enough samples to trust.
      02 · HOW IT PREDICTSExtend the trendIt draws a straight line through the recent history and extends it 5, 10 and 15 minutes ahead. Distant predictions are shrunk on purpose — the further out, the less sure.
      03 · SAFETY LIMITSCap + confidenceChanges are capped and clamped so one noisy reading can't spike the forecast. Each prediction shows a confidence level, and the sea state reads CALM, MODERATE or ROUGH.
      04 · HOW IT IS JUDGEDBeat "no change"The model must beat the simplest baseline — assume nothing changed — on real held-out data, measured with MAE, RMSE and bias. Until it does, it is a demo.

      SIMULATED / NOT A TRAINED MODEL RESULT This page shows the method, not a finished model: chronological data split, persistence baseline, MAE / RMSE / bias reporting. Accuracy and skill remain unvalidated.

      Shore-based AI target: 5 / 10 / 15-minute-ahead estimated Hs from Bay Station history, compared against the persistence baseline.

      09 / DASHBOARD, SECURITY AND HEALTH

      Complex engineering.
      A clear picture.

      This is not a picture of the dashboard — it is the dashboard itself, served by the project's own edge software. What the panel sees here is what the operator sees at the Bay Station: grouped telemetry with explicit states LIVE / SIMULATED / ESTIMATED, so no reading is ever presented as more than it is.

      SECURITY AND HEALTH

      Security states: SECURE, WARNING, ALERT, DISARMED. GPS geofence persistence, vibration / tamper detection and the enclosure switch support buoy health. Normal wave motion must not generate alerts; false-positive testing is required.
