Phase 1 · Wave estimation

Wave height, 15 minutes ahead.

The buoy's record for each 2-minute window is a lowest and a highest water level. Type a pair to get the wave height, H = highest − lowest. After eight readings, the console opens a 15-minute forecast and compares it with the reading that actually arrives. This forecast is the paper's damped linear-trend baseline — a transparent comparison method, not the trained AI model.
