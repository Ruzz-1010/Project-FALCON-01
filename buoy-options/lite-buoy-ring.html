Why a ring (vs can + catamaran)

  • Foam donut float unsinkable collar, no molding, widest waterplane of the three
  • Open moon pool waves pass through instead of lifting the hull — less heave
  • Pressure sensor hangs sheltered calm water inside the ring, easy to service
  • Trade-off: bulkiest to transport and the braced tower joint must be proven

Sensor placement

  • Wind head atop the pole slim pole straight into the hub — clean airflow
  • 3 open cups Ø110mm class compact head suits the small buoy
  • Wind vane 120mm below rotor speed + direction
  • Panel clamped on the pole single 40W-class, tilted 15°
  • GPS puck + whip on deck kept clear of the panel
