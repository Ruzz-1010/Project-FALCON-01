# REV5_LOWER_TO_MAIN_FRAME_SUPPORT_CAGE_V2

Corrects the tube overhang in V1. Four tubes start at the 365 mm lower-collar
radius and taper inward to the 336 mm attachment radius on the underside of
`REV5_MAIN_BUOY_FRAME_SUPPORT`. Their upper endpoints are below the support deck
and do not project outside or above it.

V1 and earlier incorrect cages are hidden, never deleted.
