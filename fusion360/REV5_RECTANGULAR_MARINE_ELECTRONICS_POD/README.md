# REV5_RECTANGULAR_MARINE_ELECTRONICS_POD

Creates a separate 300 x 280 x 400 mm UV-HDPE marine cabinet at the same
elevation as the earlier round pod. Chamfered 35 mm corners give a practical
rounded-rectangle envelope while retaining flat equipment-mounting faces.

Included as separate editable components:

- 8 mm hollow HDPE enclosure and base;
- removable 18 mm top-service lid with dual EPDM gasket paths;
- 330 x 310 mm ventilated rain/sun hood;
- leak tray, lower battery/power deck, and upper control deck;
- rear sealed thermal bridge interface;
- downward IP68 cable-gland plate and hydrophobic membrane-vent boss.

The old round pod is hidden only after successful generation. No completed
component is moved, cut, joined, or deleted. Verify sealing, thermal, structural,
salt-fog, vibration, EMC and flotation performance before fabrication.
