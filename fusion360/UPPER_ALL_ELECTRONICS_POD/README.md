# UPPER_ALL_ELECTRONICS_POD

Creates a separate editable upper-service assembly between the two solar-frame
sides. The 320 mm OD x 400 mm UV-stabilized HDPE pod includes an open-top shell,
double-gasket removable lid, ventilated sun/rain shield, leak tray, battery
restraint deck, thermal spreader, control rack, downward connector panel, and
waterproof membrane-vent boss.

Cooling is sealed internal recirculation with heat transfer through the thermal
spreader and pod wall. No salt-air intake opens into the dry compartment.
Existing components are not moved, hidden, or deleted.
