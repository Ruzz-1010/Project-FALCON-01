# REV5_TOWER_FRONT_MAINTENANCE_GATE

Creates a separate tapered, X-braced front maintenance gate aligned with the
front doors of the rectangular outer pod and inner sealed electronics box.

The optional inner sealed box/cooling assembly is not required to generate the
gate. The only required components are the tapered mast and rectangular pod.

- nominal opening: 340 mm lower width, 310 mm upper width, 390 mm height;
- left-side 316L removable hinge pins and four hinge barrels;
- right-side upper/lower captive compression-lock envelopes;
- 25 mm 6061-T6 perimeter tubes and 20 mm X-bracing;
- closed-position anti-rattle stops; and
- minimum intended opening angle of 100 degrees.

After successful creation, only the existing south/front rails and X-braces
inside the gate opening are hidden. They are not deleted. Side and rear tower
bracing, mast legs, solar panels, electronics pod, and sensors are untouched.

The gate remains a separate editable component for animation and joint setup.
Verify hinge pins, latch preload, fatigue, wind load, collision clearance and
safe maintenance access before fabrication.
