# REV5_POD_DUAL_SOLAR_FRAME

Fresh frame generator for the current Revision 5 layout. It does not require
any earlier upper frame. It creates two open structural side frames only (East
and West), four deck feet, lower diagonal braces, three panel rails per side,
and a compact central sensor bridge above the sealed pod.

Nominal structure: 25 mm OD 6061-T6 members, Z500–1320 mm overall, 380 mm side
spacing, and clearance for the 320 mm OD upper electronics pod. Existing
components are not moved, hidden, edited, or deleted.
