Adds separate editable packaging components for eight 316L lid clamps, four
vibration isolators, six downward IP68 cable glands, an external emergency
disconnect, a lightning bonding lug, a solar surge-protection enclosure, and a
secondary lid-retention cable. Existing pod and equipment positions are not
changed.
