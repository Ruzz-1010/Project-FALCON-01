# DUAL_SOLAR_COMPACT_FRAME_V2

Creates the visible replacement frame for the two opposed 30 W panels. The new
frame is 360 mm OD, runs from Z550 to Z1170 mm, and uses four 20 mm posts, three
slim rings, a top sensor cross, and East/West solar brackets.

The original 560 mm frame and the first 460 mm compact frame are hidden, not
deleted. Existing solar panels are not moved. Capture Position and save before
and after running.
