# COMPACT_ELEVATED_UPPER_TOWER

Corrective Revision 5 upper-tower upgrade. It creates a compact 460 mm OD,
470 mm high 6061-T6 frame using three slim rings and four 20 mm posts. The solar
panels remain at the approved +150 mm elevation, while `TOP_SENSOR_ARRAY` and
`NAVIGATION_LIGHT` are raised 150 mm to the new top deck.

The original 560 mm upper frame and temporary solar elevation struts are hidden,
not deleted. The buoy body, electronics, ballast, top cap, and vent ducts are not
moved. Capture Position and save before and after running.
