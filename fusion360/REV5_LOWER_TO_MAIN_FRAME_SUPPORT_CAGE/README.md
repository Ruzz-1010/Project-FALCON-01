# REV5_LOWER_TO_MAIN_FRAME_SUPPORT_CAGE

Creates the corrected four-tube structural connection only between the lower
buoy collar at the rounded bottom and `REV5_MAIN_BUOY_FRAME_SUPPORT`.

The tubes terminate at the main-buoy support ring/deck. They do not continue to
the tapered mast, solar panels, sensor platform, or antennas. Earlier incorrect
external-cage versions are hidden, not deleted.
