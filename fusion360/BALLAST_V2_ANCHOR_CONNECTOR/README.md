Adds separate upper/lower 316L shackles, swivel, compact elastic snubber, and
secondary safety lanyard between the adjustable ballast clevis and the existing
anchor eye. Final mooring scope and line length require site depth, tide, wave,
current, seabed, and marine-engineer validation.
