# TWO_SIDE_SOLAR_FRAME_V3

Replaces the visible circular/cage frame with two open flat frames only: one on
the East side and one on the West side for the two 30 W panels. Each side uses
20 mm round 6061-T6 tubing, two vertical posts, and three horizontal rails.
A minimal top crossbar supports the upper sensors. There are no North or South
side frames.

Previous frames are hidden, not deleted. Existing panels and equipment are not
moved. Capture Position and save before and after running.
