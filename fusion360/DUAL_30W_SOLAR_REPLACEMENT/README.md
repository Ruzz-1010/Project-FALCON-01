# DUAL_30W_SOLAR_REPLACEMENT

Creates two separate editable 30 W monocrystalline panels on the opposite East
and West sides of the compact upper tower. Each panel is 450 x 300 x 20 mm,
centered at Z1170 mm, and tilted 20 degrees outward from vertical. Total nominal
solar power is 60 W.

The former four-panel array, old tilt brackets, and temporary elevation supports
are hidden, not deleted. No buoy, electronics, ballast, sensor, or vent component
is moved. Capture Position and save before and after running.
